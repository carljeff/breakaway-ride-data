How this works

For rides with normalized power and duration, estimated TSS = hours × (normalized power / FTP)² × 100. Uses dated FTP observations where available. These are estimates, not recorded TSS: normalized power from a full ride may include cooldowns and can misrepresent intervals or races. Missing power or FTP stays missing, not zero.

Fitness and fatigue are exponentially weighted daily stress estimates with 42- and 7-day time constants. Form is their difference. Classification is deliberately conservative: missing history, changing FTP, or incomplete coverage can produce an Insufficient data label. Improving and peaking require performance validation and are not inferred from load alone. Possible excessive load is a caution, not a diagnosis of overtraining.
